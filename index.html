A proposed government-supported community learning centre providing free and accessible study spaces for students from low-income families living in overcrowded neighbourhoods.

About Our Initiative

Our proposal is to transform suitable unused public spaces into permanent neighbourhood study centres. These centres will provide students with a peaceful place to study after school, even when their homes do not have enough space.

• Government-supported infrastructure.

• Annual funding for maintenance.

• Appointed centre coordinators.

• Regular safety and cleanliness inspections.

• Free access for eligible students.
